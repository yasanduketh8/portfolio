About

I'm drawn to the parts of software that are easy to overlook and expensive to get wrong: authentication, access control, and the quiet gap between "it works" and "it's safe." That's shaped both what I build and what I study.

My background is full-stack web development — Angular, Django REST Framework, PostgreSQL — which gives me a practical understanding of where vulnerabilities actually come from, not just how to scan for them. I pair that with a growing focus on network security, vulnerability assessment, and security monitoring, and I build my own tooling to learn faster than reading alone allows.

I'm looking for a junior analyst or SOC-adjacent role where I can apply this foundation on a real team, under real mentorship.
